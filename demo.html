Demo

Demo: the prototype in action

A short screen recording of my prototype app on an Android phone, analysing thick blood smear images with no internet connection. It shows the app as it is today, not a finished product.

Demo video coming soon
The recording is being edited and will appear here.

What you will see

  • Choosing a thick blood smear image in the app
  • The app working offline, with the phone in airplane mode
  • Likely parasites circled, with a count, for a person to check
  • The confidence shown beside each mark, and where the app is still unsure
Not a diagnostic tool. The prototype is unfinished research and has not been tested in a clinic. Malaria must be diagnosed by qualified laboratory staff.

Image credit: images in this demo come from the annotated malaria image dataset of the Makerere University AI Lab (air.ug/microscopy), released with J. A. Quinn et al., “Deep convolutional neural networks for microscopy-based point of care diagnostics,” PMLR vol. 56, 2016 [7]. Used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence.
